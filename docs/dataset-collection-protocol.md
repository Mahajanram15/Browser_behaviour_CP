# Controlled Behavioral Dataset Collection Protocol

**Document Version:** 1.1.0  
**Phase:** Phase 4C — Controlled Human-Like Automation Challenge  
**Classification:** Scientific Protocol & Research Guidelines  
**Target Repository:** `Browser-to-Backend Behavioral Integrity Verification System`

---

## 1. Research Objective

The primary objective of this protocol is to establish a rigorous, reproducible, and scientifically valid methodology for collecting a **controlled research dataset** of client-side web behavioral telemetry. 

The resulting dataset is used to train and evaluate supervised machine learning models (specifically tree-based classifiers such as XGBoost) to distinguish between:
- **Class 0 (`HUMAN`)**: Interactions generated by real human participants navigating a web browser.
- **Class 1 (`AUTOMATED`)**: Interactions generated by controlled browser automation harnesses.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scientific Disclaimer**: This dataset is explicitly designed as a *controlled research dataset* captured under predefined tasks within a standardized browser environment. It does **not** claim to represent all human browsing varieties, physiological demographics, accessibility technologies, or all sophisticated adversarial bot behaviors (such as adversarial mimicry or GAN-driven agents).

---

## 2. Human Collection Procedure

Human sessions capture authentic human motor variability, neurological delays, organic trajectory curvatures, and natural pause cadences.

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Participant Briefing**:
   - The participant is briefed on the task objectives and informed that no personal data or typed keystroke content will be collected.
   - The participant accesses the standardized research interface (`/demo/telemetry`).
2. **Session Configuration**:
   - The operator or participant verifies the Collection Mode toggle is set to `HUMAN [0]`.
   - The participant enters their research identifier (e.g. `p_01`) in `Entity ID` (optional, for grouped cross-validation).
   - The participant selects an assigned task identifier (`TASK_01`, `TASK_02`, or `TASK_03`).
3. **Execution of Predefined Task**:
   - The participant clicks **"Start Labeled Session"** (binds `collection_mode = "HUMAN"`, `automation_condition = "NONE"`, `label = 0`, `task_id`).
   - The participant follows the on-screen task checklist naturally without rushing or artificially attempting to mimic a machine.
   - Keystrokes, mouse moves, clicks, scrolls, and navigations are recorded by the client-side Browser SDK.
4. **Session Termination**:
   - Upon completing the task steps, the participant clicks **"Complete & Label Session"**.
   - The SDK flushes remaining buffered telemetry batches to `POST /api/behavior/events` and signals session completion to `POST /api/session/end`.

## 3. Automated Collection Procedures

To systematically evaluate model sensitivity and robustness against varying degrees of behavioral regularity, the system supports **two controlled automation conditions**, both executing the identical research tasks:

```
Automated Mode (Label = 1)
  ├── Condition 1: AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC (Rigid baseline kinematics)
  └── Condition 2: AUTOMATED_VARIABLE      (Intermediate challenge with bounded seeded variability)
```

### 3.1 Baseline: `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC`
- **Purpose**: Establishes a predictable, machine-characteristic baseline representing standard scripted test automation or rudimentary scrapers.
- **Mouse Kinematics**:
  - Linear point-to-point trajectory interpolation from start $(120, 150)$ to target $(480, 520)$.
  - Constant sampling rate ($33$ ms / $\approx 30$ Hz).
  - Perfectly uniform velocity ($500.0$ px/s), zero acceleration ($0.0$ px/s$^2$), zero angular jitter (`direction_change_deg = 0.0`).
- **Keystroke Cadence**:
  - Fixed $50$ ms dwell duration and fixed $70$ ms flight duration ($120$ ms inter-key interval).
- **Scroll Dynamics**:
  - Uniform $120$ px increments at exact $100$ ms intervals ($1200.0$ px/s).
- **Click Dynamics**:
  - Constant $500$ ms pre-click latency.

### 3.2 Challenge Condition: `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE`
- **Purpose**: Creates an intermediate difficulty benchmark by introducing bounded, reproducible behavioral variation into kinematics and timings.
- **Mouse Kinematics**:
  - Trajectory curvature generated via a parametric quadratic Bézier curve:
    $$B(u) = (1-u)^2 P_0 + 2(1-u)u P_1 + u^2 P_2, \quad u \in [0, 1]$$
    where control point $P_1$ introduces a perpendicular normal offset ($\pm 25$ to $45$ px).
  - Variable velocity following a bell-shaped acceleration/deceleration profile with bounded perturbations ($120$ to $850$ px/s).
  - Realistic non-zero direction changes ($\Delta \theta > 0^\circ$).
  - Bounded sampling jitter ($28$ to $42$ ms per step) and a controlled mid-trajectory pause ($40$ to $75$ ms).
- **Keystroke Cadence**:
  - Dwell times vary boundedly around baseline: $[42, 68]$ ms.
  - Flight times vary boundedly around baseline: $[55, 95]$ ms.
  - Inter-key intervals dynamically match dwell + flight.
- **Scroll Dynamics**:
  - Scroll distances vary between $90$ and $160$ px per burst.
  - Scroll intervals vary between $80$ and $140$ ms ($s_{\text{vel}} = \Delta y / \Delta t$).
- **Click Dynamics**:
  - Pre-click delay varies boundedly between $80$ and $160$ ms; inter-click interval varies between $400$ and $650$ ms.

### 3.3 Why the Second Condition Exists
In initial baseline experiments, `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC` resulted in near-trivial separability ($F_1 = 1.000$) due to zero acceleration and zero direction changes. In real-world security scenarios, bots rarely exhibit pure zero variance—even simple headless scripts introduce network latency jitter and variable execution delays. `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` forces the ML model to rely on complex multidimensional behavioral distributions rather than single degenerate zero-variance features.

### 3.4 Reproducibility Mechanism
- All variation in `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` is driven by a seeded pseudo-random generator: `random.Random(seed)`.
- Given the same integer seed (e.g. `seed=42`), the generator produces the exact identical sequence of event coordinates, timestamps, and kinematic payloads.
- Experiments can be reproduced down to the millisecond across different systems without non-deterministic drift.

## 4. Task Definitions

To prevent the classifier from confounding task types with behavioral classes, **all three collection conditions (`HUMAN`, `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC`, `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE`) execute the exact same canonical tasks**:

### `TASK_01`: Catalog Browsing & Product Inspection
- **Goal**: Simulate an e-commerce visitor browsing a store catalog and inspecting product details.
- **Routes Traversed**: `/demo/landing` $\rightarrow$ `/demo/catalog`
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Inspect landing page hero zone.
  2. Navigate to product catalog.
  3. Scroll through product listings.
  4. Click on a product card to inspect specifications.
  5. Scroll specifications.
  6. Return to catalog and home page.

### `TASK_02`: Search & Query Filtering
- **Goal**: Simulate a user searching for items and inspecting returned search results.
- **Routes Traversed**: `/demo/search`
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Navigate to search interface.
  2. Click search input box.
  3. Enter query (timing metadata only: dwell, flight, inter-key interval).
  4. Click search result card.
  5. Scroll down to read details.
  6. Return to search results.

### `TASK_03`: Multi-Page Exploration & Form Interaction
- **Goal**: Simulate multi-step navigation across distinct views combined with form interaction.
- **Routes Traversed**: `/demo/landing` $\rightarrow$ `/demo/catalog` $\rightarrow$ `/demo/cart` $\rightarrow$ `/demo/checkout`
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Explore landing page.
  2. Navigate to catalog and toggle filter options.
  3. Navigate to cart checkout step.
  4. Focus form inputs and enter sample values.
  5. Scroll through checkout summary.
  6. Navigate to confirmation.

## 5. Label Definitions & Authoritative Mechanism

| Collection Mode | Automation Condition | Label Value | Class Meaning | Source of Label |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| `HUMAN` | `NONE` | `0` | Human User | Collection process context |
| `AUTOMATED` | `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC` | `1` | Automated Agent | Baseline automation harness |
| `AUTOMATED` | `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` | `1` | Automated Agent | Variable challenge harness |

### Non-Inference Guarantee:
- **Labels strictly originate from the ground-truth generation context.**
- The system **never** infers, predicts, or alters labels based on extracted behavioral features.
- Both deterministic and variable automated sessions receive authoritative `label = 1`.

---

## 6. Privacy Rules & Zero PII Guarantee

The collection pipeline enforces strict privacy guarantees by design:

1. **Zero Keystroke Storage**:
   - Neither typed characters, ASCII codes, virtual key codes, nor string values are ever recorded, buffered, or transmitted.
   - Telemetry captures only statistical time differentials: `dwell_ms`, `flight_ms`, `inter_key_interval_ms`, and boolean flags (`is_backspace`, `is_paste`).
2. **Zero Form Data Capture**:
   - Form field values, passwords, emails, addresses, and clipboard contents are completely excluded.
3. **Identity Anonymization**:
   - User IP addresses are pseudonymized at ingestion via SHA-256 hashing with salt (`ip_hash`).

---

## 7. Metadata Rules & Decoupling

Metadata fields are vital for provenance and evaluation splitting, but **must never be introduced into the machine learning feature space**.

### Disallowed ML Features:
The following metadata fields must **NEVER** appear in `features.csv` or model input vectors:
- `session_id`
- `collection_mode`
- `automation_condition`
- `participant_id`
- `task_id`
- `collection_timestamp`
- `user_agent`
- `ip_hash`
- `browser_driver`
- `process_id`
- `is_automated`

## 8. Dataset Structure

The export engine generates two physically decoupled CSV files inside `ml/data/`:

```
ml/data/
├── features.csv     # 76 behavioral features + 1 target label (77 columns)
└── metadata.csv     # Provenance, condition & participant tracking (8 columns)
```

### `features.csv` Schema:
```csv
mouse_event_count,mouse_total_distance_px,mouse_mean_velocity,...,nav_back_click_count,label
142.0,1850.4,320.5,...,0.0,0
85.0,920.0,500.0,...,0.0,1
```
- Contains **strictly 76 numerical behavioral features** followed by the target column `label` (`0` or `1`).

### `metadata.csv` Schema:
```csv
session_id,collection_mode,automation_condition,task_id,participant_id,collection_timestamp,label,event_count
sess_human_001,HUMAN,NONE,TASK_01,p_01,1790756000000,0,148
sess_det_TASK_01_001,AUTOMATED,AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC,TASK_01,bot_det_01,1790756045000,1,23
sess_var_TASK_01_006,AUTOMATED,AUTOMATED_VARIABLE,TASK_01,bot_var_s105,1790756090000,1,25
```
- Tracks session provenance, automation condition, and participant entity ID without contaminating feature vectors.

## 9. Quality Checks & Acceptance Criteria

Every exported dataset undergoes automated audit via `pipeline.generate_quality_report()`:

1. **Numerical Cleanliness**: `nan_count_total == 0` and `inf_count_total == 0`.
2. **Completeness**: Exactly 76 behavioral features per session.
3. **Rejected Session Tracking**: Non-silent recording of skipped/empty sessions in `skipped_details`.
4. **Class Balance**: Audited via `class_balance` (e.g. `0.5:0.5`).
5. **Condition Breakdown**: Reports session count per automation condition (`NONE`, `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC`, `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE`).
6. **Multi-Task Representation**: Every task contains sessions from each active condition.

---

## 10. Train / Test Leakage Prevention & Grouped Evaluation

When evaluating future models, practitioners must prevent data leakage using `participant_id` and `automation_condition`:

1. **Participant Grouping (`GroupKFold`)**:
   - Multiple sessions from the same human participant (`participant_id = "p_01"`) must reside entirely within either the training set or test set—never split across both.
   - For automated sessions, entity identifiers (e.g. `bot_det_01`, `bot_var_s105`) allow grouping by bot configuration or generation seed.
2. **Cross-Condition Generalization Testing**:
   - Train on `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC` + `HUMAN`, test on `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` + `HUMAN` (or vice versa) to rigorously evaluate if the classifier learns genuine human behavioral bounds rather than memorizing specific bot artifacts.
3. **Temporal Partitioning**:
   - Train on older sessions, evaluate on newer sessions to assess stability against behavioral drift.

---

## 11. Recommended Initial Experiment Sizing

For initial challenge validation:
- **Human Sessions**: $50$ to $100$ sessions across tasks.
- **Automated Sessions**:
  - $25$ to $50$ `AUTOMATED_DETERMINISTIC` sessions.
  - $25$ to $50$ `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` sessions (varying seeds).
- **Total Initial Scale**: $100$ to $200$ balanced sessions.

---

## 12. Limitations

1. **Intermediate Challenge Scope**: `AUTOMATED_VARIABLE` introduces bounded kinematic jitter and curvature, but does **not** represent sophisticated human-mimicking malware, human-in-the-loop CAPTCHA solvers, or GAN-synthesized trajectory models.
2. **Task Specificity**: Telemetry is captured under structured research tasks; exploratory free-form browsing may exhibit higher variance.
3. **Synthetic Seeds**: Automated sessions must be tested across diverse seeds to avoid overfitting to specific pseudo-random sequences.
